# game-life

This game, which thoroughly simulates life with simple rules, can this creature be considered alive? If bacteria can be considered alive, then why not? Some live in the material world, while others live in the positive and negative charges of electrons in this computer.

Each of the millions of bacteria has its own memory, sense of smell, the ability to leave pheromones, hear, see, and produce sounds. They can also evolve and be divided into classes.
Purple ones are viruses, constantly editing their code.
Blue ones report danger.
Green ones are the working class, working for themselves.
Yellow ones are miners and always give resources to someone. If your world is filled only with them, congratulations, you have communism: pure, real, unpolished, and interesting.
